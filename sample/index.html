About Us

We specialize in both short-stay visas, including leisure, business, and family visits, as well as long-stay visas such as family reunions, study, and migration. Our end-to-end support ensures a smooth and stress-free process, guiding you through every step with care, efficiency, and transparency — from free consultations and document preparation to visa appointments and final submissions.

In addition to visa services, we offer a complete range of travel solutions, including air ticketing to all destinations, hotel bookings, and tour packages across Asia, Europe, and beyond. We also provide travel insurance and support for train passes like Eurail and Swiss Pass, as well as bus and domestic flight arrangements.

Visa World offers stress-free visa services, air tickets, and outbound tours — handled by experienced travel professionals in Sri Lanka.
